\begin{document}

The quantity we care about is
\[
E = \frac{x_i^2}{\sqrt{y}}
\]
and its partial sums converge,
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{k^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6} - \varepsilon_n .
\]
For a linear map we write
\[
A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det A = ad - bc .
\]

Inline formulae are not detected, only displayed ones. This sentence
contains $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$ so the tutorial has something to point at.
